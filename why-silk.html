Absolutely Zero Maintenance

No watering. No trimming. No feeding. No changing vase water. No sunlight requirements. Simply place your arrangement and enjoy it — forever. Perfect for busy lifestyles and frequent travellers.

~ Questions ~

Frequently Asked Questions

✿

Do silk florals look realistic?

+

Absolutely! Our premium silk florals are crafted with extraordinary attention to detail — the texture, colour gradients, and natural imperfections of real flowers are all faithfully reproduced. Many of our customers report that guests regularly mistake them for fresh flowers.

How do I care for my silk arrangement?

+

Silk florals require virtually no maintenance. Simply dust them occasionally with a soft brush or use a gentle blast of cool air from a hair dryer on the lowest setting. Keep them out of direct, prolonged sunlight to preserve their colours. That's it!

Are they really safe for pets?

+

Yes! Our silk florals are made from non-toxic, non-poisonous materials. Unlike many fresh flowers (lilies, tulips, daffodils, and others are highly toxic to cats and dogs), our arrangements pose no risk to your beloved pets. We always recommend keeping arrangements out of reach of very young animals who might chew on them, but they are completely safe.

How long will my arrangement last?

+

With proper care, our silk floral arrangements can last many years — even decades. Unlike fresh flowers that last 5–10 days, your 21 Florals arrangement is a lasting investment in beauty for your home.

Can I customise my arrangement?

+

Absolutely! We love creating custom arrangements. Simply visit our Contact page and fill out the Custom Order form with your vision — flower types, colours, size, vase preference, and occasion. We'll work with you to create something truly unique.

Do you offer wedding packages?

+

Yes! We offer a range of wedding packages from intimate ceremonies to grand celebrations. Visit our Weddings page to learn more, or contact us directly to discuss a custom package tailored to your vision and budget.
