Silk Florals vs. Real Flowers

While real flowers die within days, premium silk florals remain everlasting. Discover why switching to high-quality silk florals is a smarter, stress-free choice for your home or events.

Feature / Benefit Silk Florals Real Flowers
Lifespan Everlasting & Permanent Dies in 5–7 days
Maintenance & Care No watering, no pruning Requires daily water & care
Fresh Appearance No wilting or fading Wilts & drops petals quickly
Pet & Allergy Safety 100% Allergy-Free & Pet-Safe Can trigger allergies & toxic to pets
Value over Time Highly Cost-Effective Expensive recurring cost
