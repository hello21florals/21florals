Our Story

Founded on the belief that floral beauty shouldn't fade after a week, 21 Florals curates and designs museum-quality silk flower arrangements. Every stem in our collection is hand-selected from master botanical artisans who utilize silk polymers, real-touch coatings, and botanical precision.

The Master 21 Palette

We restrict our studio palette to a curated master list of exactly 21 premium floral species. This ensures every leaf, petal, and stem across our shop, custom orders, and installations maintains an uncompromising standard of realism, color harmony, and texture.

Sustainable Beauty

By replacing short-lived fresh flowers with permanent botanicals, our arrangements eliminate the environmental footprint of refrigeration, transatlantic cold-chain shipping, and weekly landfill floral waste.
