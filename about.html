~ How It All Began ~

A Passion Born from Nature's Beauty

21 Florals was born from a deep, lifelong love of nature and a fascination with the way flowers can transform a space — filling it with colour, warmth, and a sense of life.

The name "21" carries a special meaning: it represents the infinite loop of nature's beauty — the way flowers bloom, inspire, and bring joy in an endless cycle. Just like our arrangements, that beauty never ends.

What started as a personal passion for creating beautiful silk arrangements for friends and family quickly grew into something much bigger. Word spread, requests came in, and 21 Florals was born — a small, heartfelt business dedicated to bringing nature's beauty into every home, every celebration, and every cherished moment.

Every arrangement we create is handcrafted with love, using only the finest premium silk botanicals. We pour our heart into every single piece — because we believe that beauty should be lasting, and joy should never wilt.

~ How We Work ~

The Art of Our Craft

Every arrangement is a labour of love

✿
01

Inspiration from Nature

Every design begins with nature. We study real flowers, seasonal blooms, and natural colour palettes to create arrangements that feel genuinely organic and alive.
