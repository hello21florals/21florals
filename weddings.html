~ The Silk Advantage ~

Why Silk Florals Are Perfect for Weddings

Fresh flowers are beautiful — but they wilt, they're seasonal, and they're gone by morning. Our silk florals give you everything fresh flowers offer, and so much more.

  • ✓

    Keep Your Bouquet Forever

    Your bridal bouquet becomes a treasured keepsake — displayed in your home for years to come, looking just as beautiful as your wedding day.

  • ✓

    No Seasonal Limitations

    Want peonies in December or tulips in August? With silk, every flower is available every season — no compromises on your dream florals.

  • ✓

    Safe for Sensitive Guests

    No pollen, no fragrance, no allergens. Your guests with allergies, asthma, or sensitivities can enjoy the beauty without any discomfort.

  • ✓

    Perfect All Day Long

    No wilting in the heat, no drooping by the reception. Your florals look freshly arranged from the first photo to the last dance.

  • ✓

    Often More Affordable

    Premium silk arrangements can be more cost-effective than fresh florals, especially for large weddings — without sacrificing any beauty.
